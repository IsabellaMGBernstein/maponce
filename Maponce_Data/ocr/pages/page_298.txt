With respect to the affairs in my country we are still in the
period of expectation. The impressions are not bad. Our
Government and our people are ready to defend the inde-

ica. Our Government is organizing itself under liberal demo-

We acknowledge the value and significance of your attitude
in favor of our independence, an attitude which 1 am sure the
Japanese people share with you. Our Government greatly
rejoice over this, because it is in accordance with its purpose of
helping Japan in her civilizing undertaking, in the same way
that our Government needs the help of your Government at
this juncture. Our avowed policy in international matters
ís in accordance with the ideas of Japanese statesmen and for
