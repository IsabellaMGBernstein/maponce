¿Concederá América la Independencia a Filípinas?—Por un protectorado
definido. —Representación diplomática y consular en el Extranjero
637 Miyokojí Y ama, Kitagata, Korakigun,
y Yokohama, 31 May, 1890.
Mr. T. Shiga, Esq., a 7 ES
“Yorodzu Choho”, Tokyo.

Mr Drar Sir: 1 thank you very much for your kind letter
of the 25th ínst., in which you congratulate me for the
good prospecet which, it seems, the question of my country is
taking now. You have many reasons in infering, from the
Reuter's telegram, a happy omen. lt means that America
will recognize our independence, and give up her first strong
decision of obliging us by force to be American subject. We
do not refuse some kind of protectorate: only we want it to be
defined and explained clearly by treaty. My Government
will exert every effort in order to have its own Diplomatic
and Consular representation in foreign countries.
