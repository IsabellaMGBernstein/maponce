204. Carta a Oshikawa

Oshikawa, enfermo.—Se cifra en él la esperanza, del empréstito.—¿Cuál

fué el resultado de la junta en casa de Imamoto?—Nuestro pueblo

clama auxilio. —Sigue en la brecha defendiendo nuestra causa.
637 Miyokojí Yama, Kitagata, Korakigun,
Yokohama, September 11, 1899.
Mr. Osmikawa, Esq.,
Tokyo.

My Dear Sir: 1 trust you will excuse me if 1 wríte to you
at the first time. 1 heard that you were ill, and by Mr. David 1
sent you my best wishes for your quick recovery. 1 hope
that you are getting already well, as it is my earnest desire.

Will you allow me the liberty of asking you about the
matter of our meeting at Mr. Imamoto's home? 1 shall be
very obliged if you would let me know kindly the result of ít.

As you know my people are now ín critical situation. They
want some help in order to continue defending our rights and
cause, which ís the cause of Far East too. 1know your human-
itary sentiments and so 1 hope you will do your best to get
from some wealthy Japanese one kind of loan to supply our
present necessíties.

I beg you not to abandon us in the present circumstances.

Expecting an answer from you and wishing you good health,

Í remaín, dear sir,
Yours very truly,
Mariano Ponce.

Please present to Mr. Imamoto my best compliments.

* Yokohama, 11 de Sept., 1899.
Mr. OsHikawa, Esq.,
Tokyo.

Queripo Sr.: Confío en que V. me perdonará sí le escribo por esta
vez primera. He oído que estaba V. enfermo y por medio del Sr. David
yo le envié mis mejores deseos por su pronta mejoría. Espero que ya
se haya V. mejorado como es mi sincero deseo.
