My Dear Sir: Í am sending you, by Parcel Post, one scarf
pin, representing our flag: please accept it as a poor souvenir.

The blue, colour of the sky, means our hope ín a future
prosperity, through progress; the red means the blood with
which we bought our independence; the white represents the
peace which we wish for ours and foreign countries. The
sun represents the progress, and some times means that the
Philippine nation belongs to Oriental family, like Japan, Korea,
etc., who bear also one sun in their flags. The three stars are
the three great groups of islands composing the Archipelago,
viz., the Luzon group, the Bisayas group and the Mindanao
group.
