“Japan and the Pacific.” ——Traducción al castellano.—El papel del Japón
en el Extremo Oriente
637 Miyokoji Y ama, Kitagata, Korakigun,
Yokohama, 10th October, 1898.
M. Inacaxa, Esg.,
Tokyo.

Dear Sir: The kindness with which you have received me
when, accompanied by Mr. J. A. Ramos, I had the great honour
to offer you my respects, inspires me courage to dare beg of
you authorization to publish, translated into Spanish, the first
part of your important book or rather the part entitled “Japan
and the Pacific,” in one of the papers that my party has been
publishing lately in Manila.
